How it works

Three stages. Use one, or all of them.

  1. 01

    Trim

    Cut, move and join clips on a timeline.

    • Drag the edges of a clip to trim it
    • Duplicate a clip to repeat a part
    • Change speed without changing pitch
  2. 02

    Remove Noise

    Clean up a recording in seconds.

    • Background noise and hiss
    • Breaths between words
    • Harsh S sounds
  3. 03

    Augment & Effects

    Hear every change live as you make it.

    • 10-band equaliser and presets
    • Pitch, harmony and chorus
    • Compressor, reverb and echo
