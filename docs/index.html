FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Audio Augment free?

Yes. It is free and open source, with no account, no watermark and no time limit.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your own device, inside your browser. Your recordings never leave it.

Which audio formats can I use?

You can open MP3 and WAV files. Results are saved as 192 kbps MP3.

How do I remove background noise from a recording?

Upload the recording, go to Remove Noise and press Remove noise. Use the Before and After switch to compare, adjust the Noise, Hiss, Breaths and Sibilance sliders if needed, then download the cleaned audio.

How do I trim an audio file or cut a part out?

In the Trim section, drag the yellow bar at either end of a clip to trim it. To cut a part out of the middle, duplicate the clip, trim one copy to the part before and the other to the part after, then slide them together.

Can I join two audio files into one?

Yes. Use Add audio to bring in a second file, drag it so it starts where the first one ends (the edges snap together), and download the combined audio.

Can I change the speed without changing the pitch?

Yes. Select a clip and use Speed to play it anywhere from half speed to double speed. The voice keeps its natural pitch.

Does it work on a phone, and is there a desktop app?

It works in modern mobile browsers, although very long recordings need more memory than some phones have. There is also a Windows desktop app that works without an internet connection.
